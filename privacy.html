Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who this is for. “Operator” means the person who runs this private QuickBooks Online utility. “Client” means the business whose QuickBooks Online company was connected.

What data is accessed

After OAuth consent, the utility may access QuickBooks Online accounting data needed for bookkeeping work for that Client company—for example the chart of accounts, deposits, and related transaction details.

How data is used

Data is used only to diagnose and correct bookkeeping records for the authorized Client company. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens and temporary working files are stored on systems controlled by the Operator (typically the Operator’s local machine). Tokens and secrets must not be committed to public source control.

Sharing

QuickBooks data is shared with Intuit/QuickBooks as needed to operate the API connection. It is not shared with other third parties except when the Client asks for a deliverable that includes that information.

Retention

The Operator should delete local tokens and temporary files when the engagement ends or when they are no longer needed.

Contact

Questions: contact the Operator who connected the QuickBooks Online company.
