Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy describes how the private QuickBooks Online utility handles data.

Data we access

With OAuth consent, the Software may access QuickBooks Online accounting data needed for bookkeeping corrections, such as chart of accounts, deposits, and related transaction details.

How data is used

Data is used only to diagnose and correct bookkeeping records for the authorized company. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens and working files are stored locally on the operator’s machine (or other systems the operator controls). Do not commit tokens to source control.

Sharing

QuickBooks data is not shared with third parties except Intuit/QuickBooks as required to operate the API connection, or when the client explicitly requests a deliverable.

Retention

Local tokens and temporary export files should be deleted when the engagement ends or when no longer needed.

Contact

Questions about this utility: contact the operator who connected the QuickBooks company via OAuth.
