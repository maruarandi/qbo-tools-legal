End-user license agreement

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who this is for. “Operator” means the person who runs this private QuickBooks Online utility (the bookkeeper / developer who connected the app). “Client” means the business owner who owns the QuickBooks Online company and granted access.

  1. This utility is private. It is not sold as a public product and is not listed for general use on the QuickBooks App Store.
  2. The Operator may connect it only to QuickBooks Online companies the Operator owns, or to a Client’s company after the Client has authorized access (for example through QuickBooks accountant access and/or OAuth consent).
  3. The utility may read and update accounting records needed for bookkeeping cleanup (such as deposits and related transactions) only as directed by the Operator for that authorized company.
  4. The Operator is responsible for reviewing changes in QuickBooks Online. The Client remains responsible for their own books and for deciding what corrections are approved.
  5. The utility is provided “as is,” without warranty of any kind.
